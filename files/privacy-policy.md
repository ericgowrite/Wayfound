# Privacy Policy

**ViyaWay**

*Last Updated: May 5, 2026*

---

## Overview

ViyaWay ("we," "our," or "the app") is a travel discovery application that helps users find travel experiences matched to their personality and preferences. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

---

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

- **Profile Information:** Name, Enneagram type, travel style preferences, and axis weights you set or we determine through the travel style assessment
- **Search Queries:** Destinations, trip types, and travel criteria you search for
- **Saved Content:** Options you save, reject, or bookmark
- **Notes:** Any notes you add to saved options or trip workspaces

### Information Collected Automatically

- **Usage Data:** Searches performed, results viewed, options saved or rejected, time spent on results
- **Device Information:** Browser type, operating system, screen resolution (for responsive display)
- **Local Storage:** Your profile and preferences are stored locally on your device

### Information from Third Parties

- **Search Results:** When you search, we retrieve publicly available information about travel options from the web
- **AI Processing:** Your search queries and profile preferences are sent to Google's Gemini API to score and rank travel options

---

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- **Personalize Results:** Match travel options to your personality profile and preferences
- **Improve Recommendations:** Analyze patterns in saved/rejected options to refine your profile (with your permission)
- **Provide the Service:** Process searches, display results, and save your preferences
- **Improve the App:** Understand usage patterns to enhance features and fix issues

We do **not**:

- Sell your personal information to third parties
- Share your individual profile or search history with advertisers
- Use your data for purposes unrelated to providing and improving the service

---

## Third-Party Services

### Google Gemini API

We use Google's Gemini API to process searches and score travel options. When you search:
- Your query and profile preferences are sent to Google's servers
- Google processes this data according to their [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- We do not store your queries on Google's servers beyond the immediate request

### Affiliate Partners

When you click "Book" links, you may be directed to third-party booking sites:
- **Booking.com**
- **Viator**
- **GetYourGuide**
- **Tripadvisor**
- **Hotels.com**

These partners have their own privacy policies governing your interactions with their sites. We may earn a commission from bookings made through these links, but this does not affect the price you pay.

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage

Your profile, preferences, and saved options are stored locally on your device. We do not maintain user accounts or store your data on our servers.

### Data Retention

- **Local Data:** Remains on your device until you clear it or uninstall the app
- **Search Logs:** We may retain anonymized, aggregated usage data to improve the service
- **No Cloud Backup:** Currently, your data is not synced to the cloud (this may change in future versions with your consent)

### Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information during transmission to third-party APIs. However, no method of electronic transmission is 100% secure.

## Your Rights and Choices

### Access and Control

- **View Your Data:** Access your full profile and saved options within the app
- **Edit Your Profile:** Modify your type, preferences, and axis weights at any time
- **Delete Your Data:** Clear all local data through your browser/app settings

### Opt-Out Options

- **Calibration:** You can decline automatic profile adjustments when prompted
- **Tracking:** You can clear your search and save history at any time
